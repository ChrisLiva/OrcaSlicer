# Convert a pre-supported model's tips, not its skeleton

Converting a pre-supported model keeps only each artist tip's contact site, approach axis and a size class, and the
Tree Scaffold builder routes its own pillars, bridges, braces and pad from those points. We did not rebuild the
artist's trunks, branches and braces with scaffold diameters, as Resin2FDM does by thickening them, although the
builder's primitives are public and would allow it. The artist sized that skeleton for resin: a figure hanging from
the build plate under peel force, with supports rooted on the figure and a raft. FDM pillars stand on the bed and take
nozzle drag, and the builder's bridge-length and brace-slenderness rules already encode that; hand-placed artist
geometry would also skip the builder's clearance checks.
